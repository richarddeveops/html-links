HTML LINKS

The anchor element connects pages and locations: <a href="page.html">Open page</a>. Its href attribute gives the destination. Relative URLs point to files in this project; absolute URLs point to other sites. A fragment link such as href="#contact" moves to an element with id="contact".

Email and telephone links use mailto: and tel: URLs. Link text should describe the destination so it is useful when read on its own. Links are for navigation; buttons are for actions.

I practised About, Services, Contact, and FAQ pages, plus navigation back to the top. The portfolio assignment is complete when each page can reach the others and the contact details work.
